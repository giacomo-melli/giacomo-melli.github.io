Research

My work concerns the political consequences of social stratification: how socio-economic inequality shapes political dispositions, how those dispositions pass from parents to children, and how the conditions under which a generation comes of age leave a mark that outlasts them. My doctoral thesis, The Bond That Lasts?, asks why class inequality in British turnout has proved so durable, and traces much of the gap to stratified experiences of political socialisation. I am now testing the argument comparatively.
